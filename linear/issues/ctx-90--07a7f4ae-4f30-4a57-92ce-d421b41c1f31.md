## What was going wrong

The **404 for** `app.coderabbit.ai.png` on `cache.agilebits.com` is almost certainly **1Password (or similar)** trying to fetch a **site icon for the CodeRabbit page**, not your MCP server. That URL pattern is their “rich icons” CDN, not ctx|.

The real product gap was different: **MCP** `initialize` already advertised icons, but they were **SVG-only** (plus a URL to the SVG). Many embedded UIs **ignore SVG or data URIs** and only use the first raster `icons[]` entry—so they showed a **generic** image instead of your mark.

## What we changed
