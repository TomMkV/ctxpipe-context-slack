# CTX-141: Close open signup on  `app.ctxpipe.ai`

Auth exists. Provisioning does not. A Gmail and the public URL is enough to create an account and get a seat. That is how waitlist junk becomes a tenant, and how a forwarded link bypasses early access.

**Why now**

We already nudge users to not use Gmail on the waitlist, but we can still get them in the app. The marketing form is not the control. Signup is.

**Direction**

Keep sign-in public. Stop uninvited sign-up from creating a usable seat.

Thinnest options, pick one:

* Disable public signup; invite or allowlist only
* Signup allowed, but no workspace / no access until you approve the email
* Signup only with an invite token

Do not build SSO for this.

**Acceptance criteria**

* Existing users can still sign in
* An uninvited account (including a fresh Gmail) does not get a seat
* Forwarding `https://app.ctxpipe.ai` does not provision the recipient
* Marketing EA form unchanged: Gmail can still submit; you still choose who gets in

**Out of scope**

* Blocking Gmail on the waitlist form
* CAPTCHA on ctxpipe.ai
* Rotating or hiding the app hostname
* Enterprise IdP
