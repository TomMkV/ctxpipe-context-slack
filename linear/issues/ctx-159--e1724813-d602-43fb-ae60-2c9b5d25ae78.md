# CTX-159: Stop creating a default team during organisation setup

New organisations get a default "general" team even when the customer said they only want repositories. The empty team shows up in the sidebar and confuses the first-run checklist.

## Acceptance

* Onboarding can skip default team creation.
* Existing orgs are unchanged.
